# Owner compliance information required

Give this checklist to the owner/director. The website cannot truthfully identify the contracting business or finalise its legal documents until these answers and documents are supplied.

## 1. Legal identity — required first

- Full registered legal name (not only “Zimbabwe Shipping”).
- Business type: limited company, partnership or sole trader.
- Companies House number, if incorporated.
- Registered office and actual trading address.
- VAT number and VAT status, if applicable.
- Owner/director/legal representative’s full name and authority to approve the terms.
- Exact legal entity that invoices UK customers, Irish customers and Zimbabwe recipients.
- Official privacy email and complaints email. Confirm whether `info@zimbabweshipping.com` is monitored every working day.

## 2. Shipping contract decisions

- When a booking becomes binding: online submission, staff approval, collection, or payment.
- Complete price list and every possible extra charge.
- Payment deadline and accepted payment methods.
- Cancellation/refund rules before collection, after collection and after departure.
- Whether customers expressly request service to begin inside any cancellation period.
- Failed collection, failed delivery, storage and return charges.
- Current transit estimates and any service that genuinely has a guaranteed date.
- Prohibited/restricted-goods list and inspection/disposal process.
- Claims deadline, evidence required and claims contact.
- Governing-law preference and the complaint/ADR scheme, if any.

## 3. Insurance — documents, not verbal assurances

- Current insurer and policy number.
- Policy schedule/certificate and expiry date.
- Standard cover per shipment and maximum cover.
- Exclusions, excess, declared-value rules and high-value cover price.
- Confirmation the policy covers the actual UK/Ireland-to-Zimbabwe route and subcontractors.

Do not advertise “comprehensive insurance” or a coverage figure until the policy documents prove it.

## 4. Data-protection registrations and advisers

- ICO registration number or written evidence that the business is exempt from the UK data-protection fee.
- POTRAZ data-controller licence/registration and any cross-border-transfer authorisation, or Zimbabwe counsel’s written advice that it is not required.
- Whether an EU establishment exists. If not, advice on whether an EU GDPR Article 27 representative is required for Irish customers.
- Name/contact of the person responsible for privacy complaints and breach response.
- UK/Irish consumer and privacy solicitor, Zimbabwe counsel, and accountant who will approve the final retention periods.

## 5. Supplier contracts and international transfers

Provide the signed/current terms, data-processing agreement and subprocessor/transfer material for:

- Supabase;
- OpenAI;
- Anthropic;
- Twilio and WhatsApp/Meta;
- Stripe and PayPal;
- Apple and Google sign-in;
- freight, collection, customs and last-mile delivery partners; and
- any email, SMS, accounting, analytics or support provider not listed above.

The owner must approve which providers receive customer documents and whether each may use data to train its models. Training on company/customer content should be disabled where the service permits it.

## 6. AI and WhatsApp operating decisions

- Which telephone numbers will use AI and which departments they represent.
- Staff coverage and maximum human-handoff response time.
- Actions Zimmy may perform without approval.
- Actions that always require a person: refunds, payment adjustments, claims, disputes, special prices, legal complaints and safety issues.
- Approved knowledge sources, escalation wording and emergency contacts.
- Confirmation that production use will not begin while using the Twilio trial/sandbox as if it were a production channel.

## 7. Retention and deletion approval

Approve or correct these proposed operational periods:

- AI analytics: 12 months.
- Closed WhatsApp/support conversations: 24 months.
- Rejected/cancelled quotes: 24 months.
- Temporary private WhatsApp invoice files: 2 days.
- Driver delivery-proof photos: 48 hours after verified delivery unless a claim is active.
- Security/audit logs: 24 months unless an incident is active.
- Shipment, customs, invoice and payment records: normally up to 7 years where justified by tax, claims or accounting requirements.

The accountant/solicitor must confirm which records actually require seven years. The business should not retain every customer field merely because the accounting record is retained.

## 8. Staff documents

- Staff/driver privacy notice.
- Employment policy covering location, route monitoring, photographs and audit logs.
- Staff confidentiality and acceptable-use agreement.
- List of staff roles and the minimum system access each role needs.
- Training record for privacy, phishing, account sharing, complaints, data breaches and AI use.

## 9. Owner sign-off pack

Return the information above together with signed approval of:

1. Privacy Notice.
2. Terms and Conditions.
3. Retention schedule.
4. AI/WhatsApp data-protection impact assessment.
5. Staff-location data-protection impact assessment.
6. Data-breach response plan.
7. Consumer complaints and data-protection complaints procedure.
8. Insurance and claims wording.

Record the approver, date, document version and next annual review date. Legal compliance is an ongoing company responsibility; deploying code does not by itself certify compliance.

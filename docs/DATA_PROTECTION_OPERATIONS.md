# Data protection operating procedure

## Privacy requests and complaints

1. Route mail with “privacy”, “data request”, “delete” or “complaint” to the nominated privacy owner.
2. Log the received date, requester, scope, identity-verification step, owner and deadline.
3. Acknowledge a privacy complaint within 30 days. Handle rights requests without undue delay and within the applicable statutory period.
4. Search the website/app account, shipments, addresses, quotes, invoices, payment proofs, support, WhatsApp, Zimmy analytics, audit records and private storage.
5. Apply any lawful retention exception narrowly; explain what was retained, why and for how long.
6. Record the response, decision, deletions/corrections performed and regulator-escalation information.

## AI and WhatsApp

- Identify Zimmy as AI at the beginning of each new channel conversation.
- Honour “agent”, “human” or equivalent immediately and pause AI replies.
- Never let AI finally approve a refund, claim, dispute, payment adjustment, legal complaint, special price or other significant decision.
- Do not paste unrelated customer records into prompts. Use the minimum fields needed for the task.
- Staff must check extracted invoice/delivery-note data against the source before accepting it.

## Incidents

1. Preserve evidence and stop further exposure without destroying logs.
2. Notify the privacy owner and technical owner immediately.
3. Record affected people, data, systems, countries, likely consequences and mitigation.
4. Assess notification obligations promptly, including the 72-hour UK/EU regulator window where risk exists.
5. Notify affected people when legally required, using clear practical protective advice.
6. Document the decision even when no notification is made.

## Monthly control check

- Review staff access and remove leavers.
- Review open human-handoff conversations and privacy requests.
- Confirm the nightly retention cleanup succeeded.
- Check private storage for orphaned documents.
- Test account deletion using a non-production test account.
- Review failed authentication, AI and webhook logs for unusual activity.

## Annual review

Reconfirm the legal entity, registrations, insurance, vendor contracts, transfer safeguards, privacy notices, retention schedule, DPIAs, staff training and app-store disclosures. Repeat sooner after a new country, provider, AI model, data category or business process is introduced.

# Draft DPIA — staff and driver location/job evidence

Status: **Owner/HR/legal approval required before routine monitoring**
Review date: 23 August 2026

## Scope and purpose

The staff app may use foreground location during an active collection or delivery job to order stops, open navigation and record operational evidence. It also records job photographs, QR verification, timestamps, route assignment and audit events. The app does not request background location.

## Necessity and proportionality

- Request location only when the driver uses a location-dependent job feature.
- Do not use location to monitor staff outside an active shift/job.
- Provide a usable manual/navigation alternative where reasonably possible.
- Show the platform permission purpose and keep the staff privacy notice accessible in the app.
- Limit customer/job records to the assigned driver and authorised operations roles.
- Remove routine delivery-proof photos within 48 hours after verified delivery unless a documented claim/incident hold applies.

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Off-duty surveillance | No background permission; active-job purpose only; written staff policy |
| Excessive manager access | Role-based access, monthly access review, remove leavers immediately |
| Photos include unrelated people/property | Staff training, minimum framing, retake/delete process |
| Location or route exposes driver safety | Restrict access, secure sessions, do not publish live route data |
| Employment decisions based on incomplete data | Human review, allow staff explanation/correction, preserve relevant audit context |
| Evidence retained indefinitely | Automated proof retention and documented incident/claim holds |

## Required consultation and approval

The owner must consult affected drivers/staff, provide the final staff privacy/monitoring policy before use, document the employment lawful basis, and confirm whether any local employment consultation is required.
